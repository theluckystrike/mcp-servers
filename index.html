Reading the KPIs as a specialist

  • Hosted activation is the leading indicator. Registries and directories only count hosted use, and an anonymous token is the cheapest possible first call. Watch anon tokens and tenant documents before downloads.
  • Tool reach beats tool count. A server that is correct on direct probes but loses to a built-in reader delivers nothing; the first-prompt reach rate is the number to protect, and descriptions are its lever.
  • Probes contaminate funnel metrics. Validation runs create checkout sessions and Pro tenants; since 2026-09-03 they are tagged (metadata.probe, signed keys) and excluded. Pre-tag sessions are excluded by timestamp.
  • Latency has a cliff, not a curve. Round 4 measured first-prompt misses when connect plus tools/list ran past about a second; p50 is tracked against 800 ms.
  • Until paid sessions are non-zero, every other number is a proxy. The KPI that closes the loop is paid sessions and minted keys; the rest exist to explain why it is still zero.
