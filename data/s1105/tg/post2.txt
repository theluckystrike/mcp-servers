Three more free MCP servers went live today, for money questions that change every few weeks.

- Social Security COLA 2027 gives the 3.6% raise for 2027, full retirement age by birth year and the 2027 earnings limits, from SSA.
- I Bond Rate gives the 5.51% rate for bonds bought November 2026 to April 2027, and what your own bond earns by its issue month.
- Mortgage Rates Today gives this week's Freddie Mac average for 30 year and 15 year fixed loans.

Each answer names its source and date. They are free, with no key and no sign-up.

https://www.mcpstamp.com/free-servers/
