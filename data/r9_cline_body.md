### Additional Information

**zovo.one MCP Server Suite — 47 production MCP servers for freelancers and small businesses.**

The repository hosts 47 MCP servers covering business paperwork: invoicing, PDF, DOCX, time tracking, expense tracking, kanban, quotes, currency, timezone, resume, asset register, and dozens more. The full catalogue is at https://mcp.zovo.one.

**Zero-install remote endpoints.** Every server is served as a remote streamable-http MCP endpoint at https://mcp.zovo.one/mcp/<server> — connect in Cline without cloning or installing anything. A free tier applies per server, with higher limits available at $19/server (one-time, lifetime) or $39 for the whole bundle.
