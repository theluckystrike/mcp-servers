   next local midnight instead of writing hour 24.

## insight

The RFC's "invalid dates are skipped" rule for MONTHLY is the difference between a correct expander and a
plausible one, and it is invisible unless a test crosses a short month: a naive `setMonth` on 31 January produces
3 March, so a monthly invoice reminder silently moves into the next month and stays wrong for the rest of the year.
The same class of bug hides in DST: expanding a recurrence by adding 7*86400000 ms keeps the instant and moves the
wall clock, so a Warsaw 10:00 weekly meeting becomes 11:00 on 2026-03-29 and never comes back. Both are fixed by
recurring on the wall clock and resolving each occurrence to an instant separately -- 2 of the 26 unit tests, and
the only two that would have shipped broken.
