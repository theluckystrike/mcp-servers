## 2. THE ARITHMETIC

Revenue = Visits x Conversion x Price.
Visits(organic) = 0. Conversion = 0. Therefore revenue = 0, and **no amount of
conversion or pricing work changes the product**. Traffic is the binding constraint.

Marginal value of publishing MCP server #59 today = **0**, because Google indexes
0 of the 299 URLs already published. Adding supply to a surface that indexes 0%
of it is a null operation. This is the single most important correction to the
standing "publish more servers" instinct.

Corollary: the only places an MCP server gets *organic* discovery in 2026 without
organic search are the registries and the awesome-lists — surfaces with their own
audiences and their own inbound links. 53 of 74 are dark.
